# User guide

**English** · [Українська](user-guide.uk.md)

Zero Waste Fridge helps your family see what is in the fridge, use food before it spoils and buy only what you need. The interface is in Ukrainian; the names of buttons below are given in Ukrainian with a translation.

## Getting started

1. Open the site and choose **«Відкрити кабінет»** (Open the app), or go straight to `/lms/`.
2. **Register** (tab «Реєстрація»): name, email, password of at least 8 characters. A household «Сім'я <your surname>» is created for you, and you are its owner.
3. Next time **sign in** (tab «Вхід»). «Запам'ятати мене» (Remember me) keeps you signed in for 30 days; otherwise the session ends after 12 hours of inactivity or when the browser closes.
4. Forgot the password? «Забули пароль?» sends a one-time link valid for 1 hour. After a reset you are signed out everywhere.

Already invited to a family? Use the link from the email, or «Приєднатися до сім'ї за кодом» (Join a family by code) on the login page.

## My fridge

The home screen («Мій холодильник») lists everything that is in the fridge, the most urgent first.

- **Add a product** — «Додати продукт»: name, category, quantity and unit (g, kg, ml, l, pcs), expiry date. The storage zone and reminder are filled in from the category; you can change them.
- **Opened package** — switch on «Упаковку вже відкрито» and set the date. Opened products keep less time (milk — 3 days, yogurt — 5…); the app uses whichever date comes first and shows it.
- **Freshness badges:** «Прострочено» (expired), «Сьогодні» (today), «1 день» / «2 дні» (use soon), «Свіже» (fresh).
- **Top cards:** total in the fridge, «Скоро зіпсуються» (≤ 48 hours), expired. Click «Знайти рецепт» in the tip of the day to cook what is about to spoil.
- **Find:** search (press `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K`), category chips, sorting; switch between table and cards on a big screen.
- **Write off** — the trash icon or «⋮» → «Списати»: choose what happened — «З'їдено вчасно» (eaten), «Використано в рецепті» (cooked) or «Викинуто» (wasted) — and how much: all or part. Tick «Додати … до списку покупок» to buy it again.
- **Edit / mark as opened** — «⋮» on a row.
- **Pick products for a recipe** — tick 3–4 products and press «Підібрати рецепт».

On a phone: products are compact cards, the «+» button adds a product, and you can **swipe a card to the left** to write it off.

## Smart recipes

«Рецепти із залишків» suggests what to cook so that food does not go to waste.

- **Recommendations** — recipes that use the most urgent products first, with «% збіг запасів» (how much you already have), the number of critical products, time, difficulty, calories, what comes from your fridge and what is missing.
- **Culinary constructor** — tick 3–4 products and press **«Згенерувати ідеальний рецепт»**. The chef AI writes a recipe from exactly these products (badge «Згенеровано AI»); if AI is unavailable, the best matching recipe from the catalog is shown («З каталогу рецептів»). On a phone this is a two-step wizard.
- **Recipe page** — ingredients from your fridge with amounts, «Чого не вистачає» (what is missing, with substitutes), basics like salt and oil, and steps.
- **Missing → shopping list** — «+ У список» for one ingredient or «Усе відсутнє — у список» for all of them.
- **«Приготувати і списати»** (Cook and write off) — choose the number of portions and which products to use. The app writes off exactly what the recipe needs (the rest stays in the fridge) and counts it as saved.

## Shopping list

One list for the whole family — everyone sees changes immediately.

- **Add** an item with an optional category, store, quantity and priority. Tick it when it is bought.
- **Smart Sync** — when a product is finished or written off, it is added to the list automatically. Configure it in «Налаштувати автопоповнення» (turn off, or exclude categories).
- **Filters** — by store and «З холодильника» (added automatically).
- **«Я в магазині»** — tell the family where you are shopping; others see «Максим зараз у «Сільпо»».
- **«Додати в Холодильник (В 1 клік)»** — after shopping, move bought items into the fridge in one step (expiry dates are suggested from the category).
- **«Поділитися списком»** — share or copy the list as text.

## Family

«Моя сім'я» (from the profile menu, or the bottom bar on a phone).

- **Roles:** the owner («Власник») can do everything; a member («Член сім'ї») works with the fridge, recipes, shopping list and notifications but does not manage people. The table on the page shows exactly who can do what.
- **Invite** by email (a link valid for 7 days, only for that address) or share the family code `ZWF-XXXX-XX` (valid for 7 days; you can issue a new one).
- **Join** another family via the link or code. If you were alone in your own household, your products and shopping list move with you.
- **Activity** — who added, opened or wrote off what, and when.
- **Leave**, **remove a member**, **change a role**, **rename** or **delete the household** (owner, with password confirmation).

## Notifications

- **Bell** in the header — reminders about products whose expiry is near. Click one to open the product.
- **«Налаштування»** (Settings, in the profile menu):
  - in-app reminders on/off;
  - **daily email** with what needs attention, at the time you choose (to the minute);
  - **push notifications** to this browser (the browser asks for permission);
  - **quiet hours** — no push at night (also across midnight, e.g. 22:00–07:00).

## Statistics & Ecology

«Статистика & Екологія» (in the top menu, or the profile menu on a phone):

- this month: products saved and wasted, the share of saved food, estimated CO₂e that did not end up in the atmosphere (and the same in car kilometres), kilograms of food;
- saved vs wasted by month (3, 6 or 12 months) and by category;
- what spoils most often, with a tip;
- who in the family saved the most this month.

Weights and CO₂e are estimates based on typical product weights and average category footprints.

## Profile menu

Click your avatar in the top right: your family and role, «Моя сім'я», «Приєднатися до сім'ї», «Налаштування», statistics (on a phone), **theme** — light, dark or as on your device (saved in your profile and used on all your devices) — and «Вийти» (sign out).

## Tips

- Everything works on a phone; you can add the site to the home screen from the browser menu.
- Changes made by family members appear without reloading; the shopping page shows whether live sync is on.
- Without internet you see the last loaded data and a banner; changes are sent when the connection is back.
- The app can be used with the keyboard only; «Перейти до вмісту» is the first Tab stop.
